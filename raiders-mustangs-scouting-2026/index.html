01 · SHOTGUN: COUNT THE WRs

No WR means run. WRs out means pass.

Zero or one receiver split out: run, 12 for 12. Two or three out: pass, 6 for 7. Their straight drop-back passing went 0 for 4.

02 · UNDER CENTER: COUNT THE BACKS

One back is play action. Three backs is a run.

One back behind the quarterback: play action every time, and 3 of the 4 went for 10 yards or more. Safeties stay deep. Three backs: an inside run every time, stopped at 0.7 yards a carry. Linebackers fill fast.

03 · TRIPS MEANS SCREEN

Three WRs to one side? Get to them.

Both times they lined up in trips it was a screen to the receivers, and one went for a touchdown. The corner and outside linebacker on that side attack the receiver the moment the ball comes out.

Our defensive jobs

  1. Everybody: count before the snap. In the shotgun, count the WRs. Under center, count the backs. Call it out.
  2. No WR, or one WR: run. Linebackers trigger downhill. Ends keep the ball inside.
  3. Two or three WRs: pass. Defensive backs play the receivers. In trips, the corner and outside linebacker on that side jump the screen.
  4. Under center, one back: play action. Safeties do not bite on the fake. Stay deep and keep everything in front.
  5. Under center, three backs: inside run. Fill your gap and tackle. It has gone nowhere.

Practice the calls: “No receivers, run!” “One back, play action!” “Trips, screen!”
