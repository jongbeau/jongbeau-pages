Find the inside linebackers.
Be ready for the middle blitz.

Green Wave lines up with four down linemen, two inside linebackers, two outside linebackers, two wide cornerbacks, and one deep safety. Green Wave frequently sends its inside linebackers (ILBs) toward the line at the snap. Most of the identified ILB blitzes bring both inside linebackers. The outside linebackers (OLBs) generally come forward to contain the edge, read the play, and react.

Inside linebackers: watch for the blitz.
Outside linebackers: expect contain and reaction.
01 · FIND THEM

Look inside before the snap.

Find both inside linebackers behind the defensive line. If they walk up close to the line, alert your teammates: “Inside pressure!”

02 · TALK

Be ready for two.

Both ILBs often come together, but sometimes only one comes. The center or QB calls out the blitz. Everyone points to the defender they are picking up so we know every rusher is accounted for.

03 · FIND THE EDGE PLAYER

The outside isn’t automatically open.

The OLBs are watching the play and protecting the edges. Expect them to meet an outside run and react to a fake.

Our plan: call it, point it, pick it up.

  1. Center or QB: identify the blitz. Find the inside linebackers and call out whether one or both are coming.
  2. Everyone: point to your pickup. Before the snap, point to the defender you are responsible for blocking. Make sure we have accounted for every rusher.
  3. Both ILBs coming? Assign the extra blocker. One of our backs or the tight end needs to pick up the free rusher. Name that player before the snap so two blockers do not take the same defender and leave someone free.

Practice the call: “Both inside! Who has the extra?” Then point out the pickups.

Coaches: set the back or TE’s responsibility within each play’s blocking rules. On pass plays, that player must stay in to block rather than release into a route.

Recognize the blitz look.

The ILBs walk up close to the line. When both come, account for six rushers through the front.

In this report, “blitz” means an inside linebacker attacking at the snap. An OLB coming forward to contain and read the play does not count as a blitz.
