What to prepare for

Under-center, run-first offense with an I-formation base. Their core is HB power and off-tackle runs, with tosses stretching the field and play-action QB keepers attacking away from the fake. Short passes complement the run action. Your observation is that the QB is under center on every play you reviewed; treat earlier shotgun drawings as suspect.

Practice priorities

  1. Align to the overloaded surface. Identify the extra TE and reset strength after motion without leaving the weak side uncovered.
  2. Fit the interior run first. Rehearse repeated iso/off-tackle calls from the I formation, especially inside the five.
  3. Set the edge against toss to the long side. Match your usual force and pursuit assignments to the field side.
  4. Keep a clear QB responsibility after play action. Rehearse the fake-left/keeper-right and mirrored versions.
  5. Carry TE releases and defend rollout passes. Trips/overload left produced two short XP passes in this sample.

These are coaching inferences from the chart, not measured defensive outcomes or a prescribed defensive front.
