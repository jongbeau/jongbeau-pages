01 · SHIFT STRONG

Find the tight end. Shift to him.

Before every snap, find the tight end. That is their strong side, and it is where 16 of their 17 runs went. The line and the linebackers shift that way. Call it out loud so everybody moves together.

02 · WATCH THE MOTION

Motion means run. No motion means beware of pass.

If a wingback runs across the formation, it is a run: 13 out of 13, every single time. If nobody moves, look for the pass. On those snaps they threw it about half the time, 8 passes to 8 runs.

03 · TACKLE LOW

Tackle low and we all swarm to the ball.

Same as the Aggies last week: they run hard and they break arm tackles. Hit low and wrap up the legs. First man stops him, second and third man finish the pile.

Their three formations

Strong side is drawn to the right on every board. On film it comes both ways, which is why a call run left and the same call run right are counted as one play.

A. Shotgun Power TE, 2 Wingbacks either back takes the snap

21 of their 29 snaps, 72% of the offense. 17 runs and 4 passes. Motion on 13 of them.

We have seen this exact offense before. It is the Aggies. Same shotgun set with a tight end and two wingbacks, same motion across, same run behind the extra blockers. It is the formation and the play the Aggies beat us with.

The two backs stand together and the center can snap to either one. Whoever gets the ball runs to the far side, and the other back leads the block in front of him. So do not key the quarterback: key the snap. A snap to the left back means a run right, a snap to the right back means a run left. That is why every one of these counts as one play and not two.

Our defensive jobs

  1. Everybody: find the tight end and call the strong side. Point at him before the snap so we all shift the same way. The line and the linebackers slide to that side.
  2. Watch the wingbacks for motion. A man goes across, call “Motion — run!” and everyone triggers. If no motion, stay honest: half of those snaps were throws.
  3. Strong-side end and outside linebacker: set the edge. Their best play is Off Tackle Strong at 15.9 yards a carry. Squeeze it back inside to our help, do not let it get outside you.
  4. Everyone: tackle low and gang tackle. They break arm tackles like the Aggies did. Hit the legs, wrap up, and the next two players finish the pile.

Practice the calls: “Tight end right — shift right!” then “Motion — run!” Everyone points to their gap.
