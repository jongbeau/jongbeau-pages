Every number here counts points above the best player still undrafted at that position, so a starter and a reserve are on one scale, and both floor at zero — a player worth less than a free agent is worth nothing, because you would drop him and sign the free agent. Starters is all nine slots of the best lineup that can actually be fielded; anyone who cannot take the field is not eligible for one and falls to the bench, which is also where a stashed player lands. Decision quality is the weighted average of all sixteen pick grades and is the only measure that does not depend on draft slot.

VORP comes from the Sleeper/FantasyPros/FFC blend, market ADP from that same weighted blend, ECR from FantasyPros alone (9/04). Grades are rule-based and identical across all ten teams — see the method.
