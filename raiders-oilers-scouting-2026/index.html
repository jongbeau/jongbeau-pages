What coaches should know

  • Bunch means run to the bunch. Out of the bunch set the quarterback kept it to the bunch side on 9 of 10 snaps. Set the edge on the bunch side and let the back-side end squeeze.
  • Empty means follow the motion. With nobody in the backfield they either hand to the motion receiver or the QB keeps the same way, 4 of 4 times. The motion side is the play side.
  • Split backs means run to the TE side. 3 of 4 runs with a side went to the TE side. The back lined up opposite the TE takes the handoff in front of the QB and runs to the TE side. The one pass from this set was a short throw to the TE.
  • The run goes to the strong side. Count the players on each side of the ball. Whichever side has more, that is where the run went on 17 of 19 runs with a side.

  • Ball security is their weakness. 5 fumbles and 8 plays for a loss in 29 snaps. Pursuit to the ball pays off.

Rules for players

  1. Count the strong side. Before the snap, see which side of the ball has more Oilers. The run went that way 17 of 19 times.
  2. Find the bunch. Three receivers stacked on one side means the ball is going that way.
  3. Watch the man in motion. When nobody is in the backfield and a receiver runs across, he gets the ball or the QB follows him.
  4. Keep an eye on #34 first. He gets the most touches, then #7 and #1. Find him before the snap.

Player quiz

Look at the formation, pick the answer, then read why. Six questions. Do it once before Saturday and once more at warm-ups.

1

Three receivers are bunched to one side. Where is the ball going?

9 of 10 bunch plays with a side went to the bunch. The one that did not was a reverse. Set the edge on the bunch side. Back-side players squeeze and chase.

2

Two backs beside the QB and one tight end. Who gets the ball, and where does he go?

3 of 4 runs with a side went to the TE side. Watch the back lined up away from the TE. When he crosses, the ball is going behind the TE.

3

Nobody in the backfield. The slot receiver on the two-man side starts running across before the snap. What happens next?

The play went to the motion side 4 of 4 times. Whoever has the motion man goes with him, and everyone else slides that way.

4

Tight end, slot and wide receiver all on one side, one receiver alone on the other. Where does the play go?

Both snaps from this set went to the strong side: a flat pass to the back and a QB keeper. Only two snaps, so confirm it early.

5

Which Oilers number should you find before every snap?

#34 had 9 touches, the most on the team. #7 had 7 and #1 had 5. He plays quarterback and running back, so look for the number, not the spot.

6

The Oilers fumbled 5 times in 29 snaps. What does that mean for you?

5 fumbles and 8 plays for a loss in one game. Eleven players running to the ball is how those happen again.
