Last pick. Correct.

Every number here counts points above the best player still undrafted at that position, so a starter and a reserve are on one scale. Starters is the best legal lineup summed; a starter counts even below that line, because the slot has to be filled. Bench is the reserves summed and floored at zero, since a reserve no better than a free agent would simply be dropped. Week 1 ready removes anyone who cannot take the field. Decision quality is the weighted average of all sixteen pick grades and is the only measure that does not depend on draft slot.

VORP comes from the Sleeper/FantasyPros/FFC blend, market ADP from that same weighted blend, ECR from FantasyPros alone (106 experts, 2 September 2026). Grades are rule-based and identical across all ten teams — see the method.
