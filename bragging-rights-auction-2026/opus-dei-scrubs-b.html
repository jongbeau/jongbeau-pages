Slot by slot

Points over a replaceable starter at the same position — value over replacement, which means the same thing at every position. Measuring against the best player left undrafted instead would hand a receiver thirty points and charge a running back twenty-eight, because this draft cleared those positions to very different depths. Both columns floor at zero: a player below replacement is not a liability, because you would drop him. For reference, the best undrafted player at each position was Michael Penix Jr. at QB, Samaje Perine at RB, Ja'Kobi Lane at WR, Brenton Strange at TE.
