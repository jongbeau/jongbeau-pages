Starters is how far the best legal lineup projects above or below an average starting lineup in this league — the average of what the ten teams actually start at each slot, so zero is a league-average roster. Week 1 ready is the same measure with anyone who cannot take the field removed. Bench over waivers counts only reserves better than a freely available player — anyone worse scores zero, since you would stream instead. Decision quality is the weighted average of all sixteen pick grades and is the only measure that does not depend on draft slot.

VORP comes from the Sleeper/FantasyPros/FFC blend, market ADP from that same weighted blend, ECR from FantasyPros alone (106 experts, 2 September 2026). Grades are rule-based and identical across all ten teams — see the method.
